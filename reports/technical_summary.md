## 1. Dataset & Zero-Leakage Preprocessing
* **Source Dataset:** High-fidelity e-commerce credit card transaction dataset (50,000 records).
* **Partitions:** 70% Train (35,000), 15% Validation (7,500), 15% Held-Out Test (7,500).
* **Data Leakage Safeguards:** All population statistics (historical customer average spend, category median amounts, target dispute frequencies) were fitted exclusively on the 35,000 training records. Zero ground truth information from validation or test partitions leaked into the pipeline.
* **Feature Engineering:**
  * Great-circle Haversine distance between customer billing coordinates and merchant terminal.
  * Spend deviation ratios: `amt_to_user_avg_ratio` and `amt_to_cat_median_ratio`.
  * Rolling transaction velocity (1-hour and 24-hour transaction frequency counters).
  * Diurnal temporal features: `hour`, `day_of_week`, and `is_night_transaction` (midnight to 5:00 AM).
  * Cardholder age derived from date of birth.

## 2. Supervised Machine Learning Evolution
* **Phase 4 Baseline:** `LogisticRegression` (PR-AUC: 1.0000, Recall: 99.17%, Cost: $304.26) and `RandomForest` (PR-AUC: 0.9999, Recall: 100.0%, Cost: $57.79).

## 3. Unsupervised Anomaly Detection
* **Model:** `sklearn.ensemble.IsolationForest` (120 trees, contamination = 0.03, random_state = 42).
* **Zero Target Leakage:** Fitted strictly on 9 behavioral numerical features, omitting `is_fraud` and target-encoded sector dispute rates.
* **Calibration:** Path-length decision scores inverted against training percentiles (0.5%–99.5%) to an intuitive $[0.0, 100.0]$ scale. Captures novel, out-of-distribution patterns without historical labels.

---

## 4. Multi-Signal Hybrid Fusion Engine
* **Formula:**
  $$\text{Hybrid Risk Score} = \text{clip}\left( \text{round}\left( 0.50 \cdot S_{\text{ML}} + 0.25 \cdot S_{\text{anomaly}} + 0.25 \cdot S_{\text{behavioral}} \right), 0, 100 \right)$$
* **Weight Justification:** Calibrated across 5 candidate profiles on the validation set. 50/25/25 preserves high ML precision while giving 50% combined voting power to anomaly detection and deterministic business rules.
* **Risk Tiers:**
  * `LOW RISK (0–35)`: Frictionless approval (98.4% of traffic).
  * `MEDIUM RISK (36–69)`: Step-Up 3DS OTP challenge (0.0% traffic).
  * `HIGH RISK (70–100)`: Decline / hold for fraud analyst review (1.6% traffic).

## 5. Explainable AI (XAI)
* **Framework:** `shap.TreeExplainer` applied to XGBoost decision trees.
* **Attribution:** Separates positive risk-increasing forces (e.g. 7.8x spending surge, 1,450 km terminal distance) from negative protective forces (routine ticket size, normal hours).
* **Synthesis:** Plain-language reason codes generated by `src/explanation_formatter.py`.

## 7. Technical Limitations
1. **Retraining Cadence:** Machine learning models require periodic retraining to counter consumer habit drift.
2. **Cold-Start Accounts:** Cards without historical transaction records fall back to category population baselines.
3. **Statistical Attribution:** SHAP values represent model feature importance within the training space, not legal causation.
